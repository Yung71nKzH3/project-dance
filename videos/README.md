# 📁 PC Dance Video Library

Drop any dance videos (`.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mov`) into this folder on your PC!

## How It Works:
1. **Zero VR Headset Storage**:
   When you connect your Meta Quest to Cyber Groove VR, videos in this folder stream directly over your local network/tunnel to the headset.
2. **Instant Dance Routines**:
   - The game automatically detects all video files in this folder.
   - When scanned with the MediaPipe AI Pipeline, the extracted dance choreography is automatically saved here as `<video-name>.json`.
   - Once saved, your Quest can launch directly into the dance routine in milliseconds without re-scanning!
